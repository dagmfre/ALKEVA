Q22. [BLOCKER] How much birr cash will be held as a float to fund user sell-backs? *

Promising instant sell-back is promising instant liquidity. Without a float, a price drop becomes a bank run.

To fund user sell-backs without risking a bank run, the platform maintains a dynamic cash float generated directly from customer buy orders. When users purchase digital precious minerals using Ethiopian Birr, these funds are held within the system. This accumulated inflow of Birr creates a natural liquidity pool, allowing the business to fund instant sell-backs for other users without the need for a separate, massive corporate cash reserve.

Q24. If gold moves 10% against the business in one week, what is the plan?

To mitigate the risk of a ten percent drop in the gold price within a week, the platform leverages advanced Al integration to monitor market fluctuations in real-time. If the value of gold declines, the system immediately notifies customers. Depending on market trends, the Al advises users to dynamically reallocate their assets to other secure digital precious minerals—such as platinum or diamonds—to cushion against losses. Furthermore, the platform can be configured to automatically alert users or trigger protective actions to stop or shift investments when a decline of a specific threshold, such as five percent or one percent, is detected, ensuring that any potential financial impact remains temporary and manageable.
